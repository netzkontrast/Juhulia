Scope: the single capsule screen (scan landing → open → redirect). Visitor mode: Experience.
Audience: party guest who just scanned the QR code from a physical gacha capsule, phone in hand, dim room.
Action: one tap on the capsule. Content: none besides the capsule; destination is external.
Constraints: black page, capsule and light only, serious register, Next.js + Three.js.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The capsule is presented like a single object in a dark vitrine: one overhead light, real reflections, real weight. It refuses the toy-gacha arrangement of candy colours, confetti and a headline.
OWN-WORLD: Pure black ground. Capsule: obsidian gloss top, satin graphite base, a hairline brushed-steel seam. Light is warm white (#F4EFE6) only: a spotlight cone, a floor pool, slow drifting dust. One small instruction line in Geist, warm grey.
STORY: The guest recognises the capsule they hold, understands it can be opened, taps once, watches it open in light, and is carried to their gift.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Capsule centred at about 55% of the width, slightly above centre, under a visible light cone; floor pool beneath; instruction line 64px above the bottom edge. The capsule itself is the primary action.
FORM: brief-pinned vitrine object, position 1 of 1 (pinned by the user). seed: none (unavailable)
Signature interaction: the seam ignites as a line of light, the lid lifts straight up, light floods out, the camera rises into it, the screen fills with light, then forwards.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
